Findbar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-21

Findbar is built to be as private as possible. The rules below are the policy, but they are also the technical reality — there is no telemetry endpoint, no account system, no cloud sync, and no analytics SDK in this app.

What Findbar does NOT collect

  • Your search queries
  • The URLs you visit
  • Your keyword list, custom shortcuts, or URL-rewrite rules
  • Any identifier — no advertising ID, no email, no account
  • Any usage analytics — Findbar has no telemetry of any kind

What Findbar stores locally on your device

Your keyword list, URL-rewrite rules, and Findbar's preferences are saved in Safari's per-extension storage on your device. They never leave your Mac, iPhone, or iPad. Uninstalling Findbar removes them.

Permissions Findbar requests

  • Search engine domains (google.com, bing.com, duckduckgo.com, yahoo.com, ecosia.org, brave.com, kagi.com, startpage.com): so Findbar can read the search query Safari is about to send and redirect it to your keyword's target.
  • webNavigation: same purpose — lets Findbar catch the navigation early enough to redirect.
  • storage: to remember your keyword list locally.
  • declarativeNetRequest: for built-in keywords, Findbar uses Safari's built-in redirect engine instead of intercepting in JavaScript. This is faster (no visible flash) and more private (Safari handles the redirect, Findbar never sees the URL).
