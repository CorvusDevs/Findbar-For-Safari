Findbar

Type a keyword in Safari's address bar, jump anywhere.

Coming soon to the App Store

How it works

g swiftui animations→google.com/search?q=swiftui+animations
gh apple/swift→github.com/apple/swift
w ada lovelace→wikipedia.org/wiki/Ada_Lovelace
!g foo !w bar→Google in current tab, Wikipedia in new tab

What you get

30+ built-in shortcuts

Google, GitHub, Wikipedia, Stack Overflow, Amazon, Reddit, npm, MDN, Translate, Hacker News, Wolfram Alpha, Wayback Machine, and more.

Edit everything

Change any default, add your own, remove what you don't use. Your list, your rules.

Multi-tab chaining

Run multiple searches at once: !g foo !w bar opens two tabs in one keystroke.

Regex keywords

Pattern-matched shortcuts for power users. gh/(\S+)/(\S+) → repo navigation.

URL rewriting

Optional, off by default: redirect twitter→nitter, reddit→old.reddit, and other privacy-friendly frontends.

100% on-device

No account, no telemetry, no cloud. Your keywords stay on your Mac, iPhone, and iPad.
